# BMSSCL League Rules Reference

Source: https://bmsscl.play-cricket.com/web_pages/full_rules

## Key Rules Summary

### Rule 7 - Points System (from 2023)
- Win: 25 points
- Abandoned/not started (weather): 9 points each
- Tied match: 15 points each

**Losing team points (batting first):**
- 75+ runs = 1pt, 100+ = 2pt, 125+ = 3pt, 150+ = 4pt, 175+ = 5pt
- PLUS 1pt per wicket taken when bowling

**Losing team points (batting second):**
- 2-3 wkts taken = 1pt, 4-5 = 2pt, 6-7 = 3pt, 8-9 = 4pt, All out = 5pt
- PLUS margin of defeat: 90%+ target = 9pt, 80%+ = 8pt, 70%+ = 7pt, 60%+ = 6pt, 50%+ = 5pt, 40%+ = 4pt, 30%+ = 3pt, 20%+ = 2pt, 10%+ = 1pt

**Weather-affected draw (side batting 2nd doesn't face equal overs):**
- 9 points each PLUS batting bonus (75+ runs scale) PLUS bowling bonus (wickets scale)

### Rule 8 - League Matches
- Played on Saturdays
- Commence at 1:00pm
- 40 overs per side
- No bowler more than 10 overs
- 20 minute interval between innings
- 30-yard inner circle: minimum 4 fielders inside at all times
- Wide ball: anything passing outside batsman's legs (normal stance)
- New ball required for each innings (minimum 2 satisfactory balls)

### Rule 10 - Cup Competitions
- Knockout basis
- 40 overs per side (can reduce to min 20 due to weather)
- No bowler more than 10 overs (pro-rata)
- Tied match: Super Over rules apply
- T20 cup from 2022 season

### Rule 6 - League Constitution
- Groups as required
- Champions: highest points in each group
- Promotion/relegation: top 2 promoted, bottom 2 relegated
- No club can have more than one team in same group (except lowest)

### Rule 15 - Score Sheets
- Results submitted by 11pm on match day
- Full scorecard on Play-Cricket by 9pm Tuesday (home club)
- Away club confirms by 9pm Wednesday
- Batting averages: min 12 innings, 250+ runs
- Bowling averages: min 25 wickets

### Rule 16 - Prizes
- Champions trophy per group
- Best batting/bowling average per group
- G.F. Terry Trophies: most runs, most wickets, best all-rounder
- Umpires Fielding Trophy
- Wicketkeeper Trophy (most victims)

### Rule 18 - Registration
- No unregistered players
- No transfers after 31st July
- Max 2 players transferred between clubs per season
- Cup qualification: Semi-finals = 1 match, Finals = 2 matches
- Second team restriction: can't play 2nd XI after 10 first team matches

### Rule 14 - Playing Conditions
- MCC Laws of Cricket apply
- 6-ball overs
- No mobile phones on field (10 penalty runs)
- White clothing required
- Must bowl 40 overs in 2hrs 30mins


### Rule 15 - Scoresheets and Averages (Full Detail)

Source: https://bmsscl.play-cricket.com/web_pages/15_scoresheets

**(a)** Both clubs (HOME & AWAY) shall submit Match Scores and any notable performances to the Results Secretary by 11.00pm on the Day of the Match, wet or fine (and not cancelled by the League) via the online Captains Card/Form.

**(b)** The Match Result/Report shall be completed on the ECB Play-Cricket site by the Home Club in respect of all Matches where the Game has started. The report shall be completed and confirmed by 9.00pm on Tuesday following the League Match and within 72 hours of the conclusion of a Cup Match. The Away Club will check (complete if the Home Club has failed to do so) and confirm the report as an accurate record by 9.00pm on Wednesday following the Match. The report shall include the full names (forename and surname) of all Players taking part (whether they have batted or not) and shall contain the full Match scores, including bowling and fall of wicket analysis.

**(c)** Averages qualification:
- Batsman: at least 12 innings AND no fewer than 250 runs in any one Group
- Bowler: at least 25 wickets in any one Group

**(d)** Fines:
- Captains Card failure: £5 per offence
- Scoresheet failure: £10 per offence + £5 after second offence

**(e)** Captain's responsibility to balance Scorebooks and agree the Score at Interval and End of Match, present to Umpires for verification and signing.

**(f)** £5 fine for failure to send in Captains Card (deemed non-submission).
