## The Situation

- We're **10th** with 4 points (1 win, 1 loss, 1 abandoned)
- **Top 4 qualify for the semi-finals**
- We need to **win** to get to 7 points
- Glusburn (currently 4th, 7 pts) are expected to lose to Riddlesden — keeping them at 7
- If we both finish on 7 points, the tiebreaker is **Runs per Wicket (R/W) differential**
- Glusburn's R/W is **+16.63** — we need to beat that
- Our current R/W is **-0.40**

---

## What This Means

This is NOT a normal game. We don't just need to win — we need to win **while losing as few wickets as possible** and **bowling them out cheaply**.

The R/W formula: (Our runs ÷ Our wickets lost) - (Their runs ÷ Their wickets taken)

Every wicket we lose counts heavily against us. Every wicket we take helps.

## Game Plan

### If We Win the Toss: BAT FIRST

### Batting Strategy

**Priority: PROTECT WICKETS above everything**

1. **Openers** — Target 80-100 partnership without loss. Play low-risk cricket for the first 15 overs. Rotate strike, no unnecessary risks. Singles and twos are gold.

2. **Set batters MUST NOT get out** — Once someone reaches 30, they stay until the end. No "having a go" and getting caught at deep mid-wicket. Your job is to be there at the end.

3. **Acceleration comes LATER** — Overs 35-45 is when set batters can open up. Not before.

4. **If a wicket falls, new batter plays steady** — No coming in trying to smash it. Occupy the crease, take easy singles.

5. **Target: 180/2 or 180/3 in 45 overs** — This gives us R/W of 60-90 for the match. That's what qualifies us.

6. **Key message: 150/2 is BETTER than 220/8** — This is counter-intuitive but it's the maths. Fewer wickets = higher R/W = we qualify.

### Bowling Strategy

**Priority: BOWL THEM OUT AS CHEAPLY AS POSSIBLE**

1. **Attack from ball one** — Ingrow are the weakest team in the competition (0 wins, R/W of -42.78). They've been bowled out cheaply all tournament.

2. **Use your best bowlers early** — Don't experiment. Put your best attack on and aim to bowl them out in 20-25 overs.

3. **Every wicket matters** — If they're 40/7, don't relax. Keep attacking to get all 10. The difference between 50/10 (R/W = 5.0) and 80/7 (R/W = 11.4) is massive for us.

4. **No wides, no extras** — Discipline. Every extra run conceded increases their R/W against us.

5. **Field aggressively** — Slips, close catchers. We want wickets, not dot balls.
