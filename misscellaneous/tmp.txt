Subject: Appeal Against Parking Charge Notices HP5085738 and HP5085739

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to appeal the Parking Charge Notices referenced HP5085738 and HP5085739.

On Sunday evening at approximately 9:38 pm, my friend and I parked at the Tesco Sale car park solely for the purpose of charging our electric vehicles using the charging facilities provided on site. We both have valid charging records/receipts which confirm that our vehicles were actively charging during the period in question.

At the time of our visit, the Tesco store had already closed (Sunday closing time being 5:00 pm) and the car park was virtually empty. We did not occupy spaces required by Tesco customers, nor did we cause any obstruction or inconvenience to any other users of the car park.

We are not local residents of Manchester and had travelled to the area to visit a friend. As we were due to return to our respective cities the following morning, we took the opportunity to recharge our vehicles overnight to ensure we could complete our journeys safely and efficiently.

It was our genuine understanding that the charging facilities were available for use and that customers actively using those facilities would be permitted to remain for the duration reasonably required to charge their vehicles. At no point was there any intention to misuse the car park or gain any unfair advantage.

Given that:

We were legitimate users of the EV charging facilities;
We have receipts confirming vehicle charging;
The store was closed and the car park was largely empty;
No parking spaces were denied to Tesco customers; and
We acted in good faith while charging our vehicles,

we respectfully request that both Parking Charge Notices (HP5085738 and HP5085739) be cancelled.

We would be grateful if you could review the circumstances and exercise discretion in this matter.

Thank you for your time and consideration. We look forward to your response.

Yours faithfully,
